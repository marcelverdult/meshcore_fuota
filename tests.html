Am Testaufbau gemessen, im Feld beobachtet

Die Funktests am Testaufbau laufen bei SF7 und 1 dBm. Die Alpha-Prüfung nutzte am Testaufbau die Funkwerte des HanseMesh (SF8, 62,5 kHz, 1 dBm). Der Feldtest läuft im echten Mesh. Bisher läuft er nur direkt zwischen Nachbarn im Feld beobachtet (Analyzer, 13. November 2026). Als Beleg zählt der Abgleich mit den gebauten Dateien: laufendes Image und SHA-256 beider Slots. Was noch nicht getestet ist, steht unter Fragen als offen.

Measured on the bench, observed in the field

The radio tests on the bench run at SF7 and 1 dBm. The bench alpha check used the HanseMesh radio settings (SF8, 62.5 kHz, 1 dBm). The field test runs in the real mesh. So far it runs only directly between neighbours observed in the field (analyzer, 13 November 2026). As evidence, we compare the running image and both slots' SHA-256 with the built files. What is not tested yet appears under Questions as open.

Testaufbau

  • Geräte: bis zu 3 FakeTecs und 1 SenseCAP P1. Der Test „Kleineres Image“ und der Dauerlauf nehmen einen Heltec T114 dazu. Der RAK4631, der Xiao und der T096 kamen für einzelne Tests dazu. Die Tests mit Release-Paket laufen auf den FakeTecs A und C, der P1 ist Nachbar.
  • Normaler Repeater: Im Test „Über einen normalen Repeater“ ist der P1 das Relais B. Er läuft mit der unveränderten Firmware 1.18.0, A und C sind FakeTecs.
  • Abstand: rund 50 cm.
  • Funk: Die Funktests laufen bei SF7 und 1 dBm.
  • Airtime-Budget: 10 %. Das ist am Testaufbau zulässig. Bei 1 % dauert derselbe Test 10-mal länger. Im Mesh gelten höchstens 0,3 % im Code geprüft.
  • Kette A-B-C: A ist der Seed. Er ist per USB installiert und bietet das Update an. B holt es von A (Hop 1), C holt es von B (Hop 2). Alle Knoten hören sich. Ein Testfilter in Software stellt die Kette nach.

Bench

  • Devices: up to 3 FakeTecs and 1 SenseCAP P1. The tests “Smaller image” and “Endurance run” add a Heltec T114. The RAK4631, the Xiao and the T096 joined for single tests. The release package tests run on FakeTecs A and C, with the P1 as a neighbour.
  • Normal repeater: In the test “Across a normal repeater” the P1 is relay B. It runs the unchanged firmware 1.18.0, and A and C are FakeTecs.
  • Distance: about 50 cm.
  • Radio: The radio tests run at SF7 and 1 dBm.
  • Airtime budget: 10 %. The bench permits that. At 1 % the same test takes 10 times longer. In the mesh the limit is 0.3 % at most checked in the code.
  • Chain A-B-C: A is the seed. It is installed over USB and offers the update. B pulls it from A (hop 1), C pulls it from B (hop 2). All nodes hear each other. A software test filter recreates the chain.

  • Nachweislauf 1.17.1 nach 1.18.0 am 9. November: bestanden. Die Kampagne hatte 145 Fragmente. Das RAK4631 war nach 9 h 21 min vollständig. Null abgelehnte Pakete, null Signaturfehler, kein Paket ging als Flut hinaus gemessen.

Ob das in einem Netz mit normalem Verkehr überhaupt auftritt, ist nicht geprüft; dort zählt jedes akzeptierte Paket, auch die Adverts der Nachbarn offen.

  • The T096 received the FakeTec at RSSI -16. The FakeTec received the T096 at RSSI -33 measured.
  • The Xiao has bootloader 0.6.1 without OTAFIX. The T096 has bootloader 0.9.0 with the suffix -dirty. Whether it has OTAFIX is not checked open.
  • Not checked: FUOTA pull by radio, LED and button, fuota uf2, serial DFU and revert open.
Alpha check (12 November 2026, with release images and HanseMesh radio settings)
  • A node with 1.17.1-b2 pulled the campaign dc7b7cb0 (174 fragments) from a seed with 1.18.0-b3. After 12.6 h it applied it and confirmed the test boot without any action measured.
  • The node refused a manifest signed with the test key (ERR manifest signature) and stayed unchanged measured.
  • The node held a signed campaign for build 2 (dc048c3d) only as a relay. It refused it for itself (not for this node) measured. Whether build or base hash was the reason is not separated open.
  • The revert without neighbours on the release image is not tested. It needs a shielded setup open.
Trial run 1.17.1-b2 to 1.18.0-b3 (test key, night of 11 to 12 November 2026)
  • Two nodes with 1.17.1-b2 pulled the update over the air from a node with 1.18.0-b3. They checked it, applied it and confirmed without any action measured.
  • From “pulling” to “confirmed” took about 18 min. That was 14 min of radio and 2 min of waiting time, plus the swap and the test boot measured. These were test images at SF7 and 1 dBm, not the release timing.
  • The node with 1.18.0-b3 did not confirm at the first two flashes and fell back. The third flash confirmed once a neighbour sent an advert every 12 s measured. That no packet arrived in the quiet surroundings is not checked open.
Field: alpha test with testers
  • The campaign dc7b7cb0 (build 3) has run since 12 November 2026 with two tester nodes observed in the field (analyzer, 13 November 2026).
  • The analyzer hears it only directly (zero-hop) through one observer observed in the field.
